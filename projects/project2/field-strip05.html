5. LUBRICATE

Like a sword needs a wetstone, a gun needs lubrication.

Use a light coating

Use a light coating of oil, as over lubrication can attract dirt and debris to stick to your firearm. A thing coating protects better than a heavy application.

Metal on Metal

Anywhere there is metal on metal contact make sure to apply lubrication. This includes the slide, the frame rails, barrel hood, lugs, and or any moving parts that show wear.

Wipe Excess and External

Wipe down all external surfaces with a clean, dry cloth. This removes residue and oils/moisture from fingerprints that you may have missed in earlier steps.
