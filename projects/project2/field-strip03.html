3. DISASSEMBLE

When performing the disasembly it is important to make sure firearm is unloaded, a loaded firearm can cause an accidental discharge.

Safety Basics

Always keep the muzzle pointed in a safe direction and keep your finger off the trigger. This prevents an accidental discharge of firearm into something you DO NOT want a hole in.

Owners Manual

Many guns require brand or model specific disassembly. Some models require the trigger to be pullers, consult your manual for you specific firearm.

Make sure firearm is unloaded

Begin by removing magazine from firearm. If the magazine is loaded make sure to relocate it to another room. This prevents mindlessly reinsertion of the magazine into the firearm. Next make sure to check the firearms chamber by racking the slide. You will want to do this part very aggressively, to insure the round, if any is properly ejected. After then check inside the chamber with finger to confirm chamber is empty.

Parts Removal

Remove the slide. Take out the barrel and recoil spring. Seperate the frame components as needed. Taking pictures will make it easier to correctly reassemble everything later.
