1. SAFETY & SETUP

To begin working make sure your area is clean and well lit.

Clear the Workspace

Keep ammunition away from the cleaning area. Preferably, all ammunition should be in a separate room.

Work in a well-ventilated area

Working in a ventilated area will keep you from becoming sick from cleaning fumes.

Personal Protective Equipment

Wearing protective eyewear protects you from debris and solvents. Additionally, wearing gloves will protect your skin against harsh chemicals.
