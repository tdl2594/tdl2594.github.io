4. CLEANING

Cleaning your firearm can be a relaxing experience, do not forget to keep your wits about you!

Cleaning the Barrel

The barrel is where accuracy starts, so ensure to clean thoroughly. Soak a clean patch in solvent and run it through the bore using the rod until it comes out the back end. Scrub the bore to remove stubborn carbon buildup. Run solvent soaked patches throough barrel until it comes out clean. When finish run dry patches through barrel.

Cleaning the Action and frame

Use solvent on a cleaning cloth or brush to clean the inside of the slide and frame. Pay close attention to areas where dirt and debris accumulate, such as around the firing pin, ejector, and extractor. The focus should be chamber, slide rails, and trigger grouping.

Follow the Manual

Remember to always refer to the firearm's manual for specific instructions on disassembly and cleaning.
