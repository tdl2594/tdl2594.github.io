7. FINAL CHECK

Always run a physical function check before considering firearm ready.

No Leftover Parts

Verify that every pin, spring, or component is accounted for in your work area.

Cycle the Action

Rack the slide or bolt several times to confirm smooth movement without binding.

Trigger and Hammer:

With the gun confirmed unloaded, pull and hold the trigger back, rack the slide, release the trigger, and listen/feel for the audible hammer or sear reset. Pull the trigger a second time to confirm it cleanly releases the hammer.

Magazine Lock Test

Insert an empty magazine to verify that the slide locks open properly on the follower.
